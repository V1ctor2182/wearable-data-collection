MyHealth Dashboard · Last updated: April 2, 2026

1. Overview

MyHealth Dashboard ("the Application") is a personal health application that allows you to securely access, download, and visualize your own health data from your healthcare provider's electronic health record (EHR) system and your personal wearable devices. The Application is designed for individual use under the 21st Century Cures Act, which guarantees your right to access your own electronic health information (EHI).

By using this Application, you agree to these Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree, please do not use the Application.

2. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years of age and capable of forming a binding agreement to use this Application. The Application is intended for individuals who wish to access their own health data or caregivers acting on behalf of an authorized individual.

3. What the Application Does

The Application enables you to:

  • Connect to your healthcare provider's EHR system (e.g., Epic MyChart, Cerner) via SMART on FHIR and retrieve your own clinical data, including lab results, vital signs, medications, conditions, immunizations, allergies, procedures, and care plans.
  • Connect your personal wearable devices (such as Fitbit, Oura, WHOOP, Garmin, Apple Health, Samsung Health, and Google Fit) and consolidate your health metrics in one place.
  • View and browse all your collected health data through a personal dashboard.

4. Your Data Belongs to You

All data retrieved by this Application is your own personal health information. You control which data sources to connect and may disconnect any source at any time. Your data is:

  • Stored securely and accessible only to you.
  • Never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Never used for any purpose other than displaying it back to you.

5. Authorization

The Application accesses your health data only after you explicitly authorize the connection through the OAuth 2.0 or SMART on FHIR authorization flow provided by your healthcare provider or wearable device platform. You may revoke access at any time through your provider's patient portal or your device platform's connected apps settings.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest.
  • OAuth access tokens are stored securely and refreshed automatically; they are never shared with any third party.
  • The Application requests only the minimum FHIR scopes and API permissions necessary to retrieve your health data.
  • Only you can access your data through the Application.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as you maintain an active connection. You may request deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us. Upon disconnecting all data sources, your stored data will be deleted within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data the Application has collected on your behalf.
  • Export your data in its original format.
  • Revoke the Application's access to any connected data source at any time.
  • Request complete deletion of your data.

9. No Medical Advice

The Application is a personal data viewer and does not provide medical advice, diagnoses, or treatment recommendations. The health data displayed should not be used as a substitute for professional medical judgment. Always consult your healthcare provider for medical decisions.
