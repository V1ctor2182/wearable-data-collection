Privacy Policy

MyHealth Dashboard · Last updated: April 2, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how MyHealth Dashboard ("the Application") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal health information. The Application is a personal health tool that allows you to access and view your own health data from your healthcare providers and wearable devices.

2. Information We Collect

The Application collects only the health data that you explicitly authorize it to access:

CategoryData TypesSource
Clinical data Lab results, vital signs, conditions, medications, procedures, immunizations, allergies, care plans, encounters, diagnostic reports Your healthcare provider's EHR via SMART on FHIR (e.g., Epic MyChart, Cerner)
Wearable data Heart rate, HRV, sleep, SpO2, steps, activity, temperature, breathing rate, stress Your connected devices (Fitbit, Oura, WHOOP, Garmin, Apple Health, Samsung Health, Google Fit)
Connection data OAuth tokens (encrypted), connection status, last sync timestamp Generated when you authorize a data source

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to:

  • Display your health information back to you in the Application dashboard.
  • Store your data securely so you can access it at any time.
  • Sync and refresh your data from connected sources.

We do not:

  • Sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party.
  • Use your data for advertising, marketing, or profiling.
  • Share your data with anyone — it is accessible only to you.
  • Use your data to train machine learning models.
  • Analyze your data for any purpose beyond displaying it to you.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your health data based on your explicit consent, provided when you authorize each data source connection through OAuth 2.0 or SMART on FHIR. You may withdraw consent at any time by disconnecting the data source.

Your right to access your own electronic health information is protected under the 21st Century Cures Act and the ONC Information Blocking Rule.

5. Data Security

We take the security of your health data seriously:

  • Encryption in transit — All communications use TLS/HTTPS.
  • Encryption at rest — Your data is encrypted in the database.
  • Access control — Only you can access your data. There is no shared access or admin backdoor.
  • Token security — OAuth tokens are stored encrypted and automatically refreshed. They are never exposed or shared.
  • Minimum necessary access — The Application requests only the FHIR scopes needed to retrieve your health data.
  • Content deduplication — Data is deduplicated using SHA-256 hashing to avoid storing duplicate records.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with any third party, period. Your health data stays between you and this Application. No analytics services, advertisers, or other organizations have access to your information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your data is retained as long as you keep your data sources connected.
  • You may request deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us.
  • Upon disconnecting all data sources, stored data will be deleted within 30 days.
  • OAuth tokens are immediately revoked and deleted upon disconnection.

8. Your Rights

  • Access — View all data the Application holds about you, at any time, through the dashboard.
  • Export — Download your data in its original format.
  • Correction — Clinical data corrections should be requested through your healthcare provider.
  • Deletion — Request complete deletion of all your stored data.
  • Revocation — Disconnect any data source at any time through the source platform's settings or your provider's patient portal.

9. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
