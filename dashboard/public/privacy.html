1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Wearable Data Pipeline ("the Application"), operated by the Systems Engineering of Autonomy (SEA) Lab at Columbia University, collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal and health information.

2. Information We Collect

CategoryData TypesSource
Wearable data Heart rate, HRV, sleep stages, SpO2, steps, activity, temperature, breathing rate, stress Fitbit, Oura, WHOOP, Garmin, Apple Health, Samsung Health, Google Fit, Xiaomi, Polar

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Academic research conducted under IRB-approved protocols at Columbia University.
  • Correlating wearable health metrics with clinical outcomes for research analysis.
  • Improving the Application's data collection and processing capabilities.

We do not:

  • Sell your data to any third party.
  • Use your data for advertising or marketing.
  • Share individually identifiable data outside the research team without your consent.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your health data based on your explicit consent, provided through:

  • The IRB-approved informed consent process for research participation.
  • Your explicit authorization via OAuth/SMART on FHIR when connecting each data source.

5. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • Encryption in transit — All communications use TLS/HTTPS.
  • Encryption at rest — Database and backups are encrypted.
  • Access control — Only authorized research personnel can access participant data.
  • Token security — OAuth tokens are stored encrypted and refreshed automatically.
  • Minimum necessary access — The Application requests only the data scopes required by the research protocol.
  • Audit logging — All data ingestion events are logged for accountability.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share your individually identifiable data with third parties. De-identified or aggregated data may be shared in academic publications, presentations, or with collaborating research institutions as permitted by the IRB protocol.

7. Data Retention

Personal health data is retained for the duration of the research study. After study completion, data is either de-identified for long-term research use or securely deleted, in accordance with the IRB protocol and Columbia University data retention policies.

8. Your Rights

  • Access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your data, subject to research retention requirements.
  • Revocation — Disconnect any data source at any time through the source platform's settings or patient portal.
  • Withdrawal — Withdraw from the research study at any time without penalty.

9. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated to active participants. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
